# Channels

Brand formats in `brand.json.formats` take priority over this table. Platform limits change; the figures below were current when written. When a limit matters (a long caption, a long video), say in the run summary that it should be checked against the platform's current help pages.

## Visual and video formats

| Placement | Size | Ratio | Safe zone and notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stories, Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts | 1080×1920 | 9:16 | Keep text and logos out of about the top 250 px and bottom 300 px, and about 120 px on the right on TikTok and Reels (buttons) |
| Instagram and Facebook feed | 1080×1350 | 4:5 | The profile grid crops the centre to 3:4; keep the key message central |
| Square feed (Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, X) | 1080×1080 | 1:1 | |
| YouTube, LinkedIn and X desktop, websites | 1920×1080 | 16:9 | YouTube thumbnail 1280×720 |
| LinkedIn single image | 1200×627 or 1080×1350 | 1.91:1 or 4:5 | 4:5 takes more feed space on mobile |
| Facebook link image | 1200×630 | 1.91:1 | |
| LinkedIn cover | 1584×396 | 4:1 | |

## Text limits and visible length

| Channel | Limit | Visible before "more" | Practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram caption | 2,200 characters | about 125 | Hook in the first line; up to 30 hashtags allowed, 3–5 relevant ones work better |
| TikTok caption | several thousand characters | about 100 | Hook on screen, not just in the caption; 3–5 hashtags |
| LinkedIn post | 3,000 characters | about 210 | Short paragraphs; no more than 3 hashtags; links in posts may reduce reach, test it |
| Facebook post | very long | about 125 | Short performs better |
| X post | 280 characters (more for paid accounts) | all | One idea; 1–2 hashtags at most |
| YouTube title | 100 characters | about 60 | Keyword first |
| YouTube description | 5,000 characters | about 150 | Links and chapters below the fold |
| Email subject | no hard limit | about 40–60 | Pair with preview text of about 40–100 characters |
| WhatsApp message | long | the first lines | Personal, short, one link; only to people who opted in |

## Video length

| Placement | Best length | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Stories | up to 15 s per card | Make each card stand alone |
| Reels, TikTok, Shorts | 7–30 s for ads and launches | Hook in the first 2 s; design for sound off, with captions |
| Feed video | 15–60 s | Captions burned in or as a file |
| YouTube | as long as it stays useful | Pre-roll ads: the message before the 5 s skip |
| LinkedIn | 30–90 s | Professional angle; captions essential |

## What each channel is for

| Channel | Best for | Voice note |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Awareness, product moments, community | Visual first; loud register works |
| TikTok | Reach with younger audiences, demonstration | Native, human, less polished beats glossy |
| YouTube | Explainers, tutorials, search | Useful first |
| LinkedIn | Business audiences, trust, partnerships, hiring | Clear register; insight over hype |
| Facebook | Older audiences, groups, local communities | Plain and direct |
| X | News, announcements, support | Short, timely |
| Email | Existing customers, conversion, retention | Personal, one goal per email |
| WhatsApp | Existing customers who opted in, diaspora communities | Personal; never unsolicited |

## Arabic and right-to-left

- Mirror the layout: text right-aligned, logo on the right, reading order right to left (follow `brand.json.logo.placement.rtl`).
- Numbers and Latin brand names stay left-to-right inside Arabic text.
- Hashtags in Arabic work on Instagram, X and TikTok; use the ones the audience actually uses.
- Choose the variety deliberately: Modern Standard Arabic for formal and pan-Arab pieces, a dialect (for example Egyptian) for social pieces aimed at one country. Record the choice in the plan.
