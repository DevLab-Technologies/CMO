# Audio

## 1. The sound logo

- Use only the file in `brand.json.sound.logo`, unedited: never re-pitch, re-time, shorten or layer effects on it.
- Place it where `brand.json.sound.placement` says (usually the end frame only). With a cmo video template, start it at the render's `sound_logo_at` (from `render.mjs --info`).
- Once per piece. Nothing else plays over it except the tail of the bed fading out.
- If the brand has no sound logo, leave it out and say so; never write one.

## 2. Music beds

A bed is allowed only with a rights record (`brand-pack` skill, Assets and rights) that covers the use: the channel, paid or organic, the territory and the duration of the campaign.

Sources, in this order:
1. A track already in the brand pack's `assets/` with a rights record.
2. A track the user supplies, with its license. Ask for the license if it isn't given; without it the bed is not used.
3. A track the CMO generated through a connected music service (section 5), with the service's terms recorded in the rights record (`source: generated`, the service, the date, the terms URL).

Never use commercial music the user hasn't licensed, and never imitate a named artist or a recognizable song.

Mixing: the bed sits about 6 dB under speech (`--bed-gain -6`) and ducks under voice-over (`--duck`). It fades out under the sound logo.

## 3. Voice-over

- The script comes from the copywriter's file, word for word. Changing a word means a new copy version and a review.
- A recorded human voice needs the speaker's written consent for this use, recorded in the rights record.
- A synthetic voice comes only from a connected voice service, generated by the CMO (`cmo:connections` rule 3), using a stock voice from that service's library. Never clone or imitate a real person's voice, including the user's, unless the user confirms that the person consented in writing to a cloned voice for this use. Label synthetic voice where `claims.json.ai_labelling` or the platform requires it.
- Arabic and other languages: a native speaker of the variety in the plan (for example Egyptian Arabic, not Modern Standard Arabic, when the plan says Egyptian). Synthetic voices are checked by a native speaker before approval; say so in the summary if no one has checked.
- Timing: the voice-over must finish before the sound logo starts.
- The voice-over script, timed, becomes the video's captions (`captions` in the fill file) so the subtitles match what is said.

## 4. Loudness and delivery

| Destination | Integrated loudness | True peak |
|---|---|---|
| Social video (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, X, Facebook) | -14 LUFS | -1 dBTP |
| Podcast and audio ads | -16 LUFS | -1 dBTP |
| Broadcast TV (EBU R128 / ATSC A/85) | -23 / -24 LUFS | -1 / -2 dBTP |

Deliver 48 kHz stereo WAV for mixing, AAC 192 kbps inside MP4 for social. The mixer normalizes to `--lufs` (default -14) and measures the result. Render a mixed soundtrack with `--loudness off` so the renderer keeps the mix as it is; the manifest then records the loudness measured on the MP4.

## 5. Connected services

Generation (music, voice, sound effects) is only through an MCP connector the user has connected, never through a website, and it is done by the CMO in the main session (specialist agents have no connector tools). Before using one:
- check the budget (protocol section 9) and record the cost;
- record the service's license terms in the rights record;
- if none is connected, say what would be needed and use the sources that don't need one (sections 2 and 3).

## 6. The mixer

`mix.mjs` builds one soundtrack of an exact length from a bed, an optional voice-over and the sound logo, then normalizes it:

```bash
node "${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/mix.mjs" --duration 14.9 --out <path>/soundtrack-v1.wav \
  --bed <bed.wav> --logo .cmo/brand/<sound logo path> --logo-at 12.2 \
  [--vo <voice.wav> --vo-at 0.5 --duck] [--bed-gain -6] [--lufs -14]
```

- The bed is looped or trimmed to the duration and fades out under the sound logo.
- Without `--logo-at`, the sound logo ends 0.2 s before the end.
- It refuses a sound logo that would run past the end, a voice-over that would run into the sound logo or past the end, bad numbers, and an existing output file (pass `--force` only to replace a scratch file). It prints a JSON summary with the measured duration and loudness; keep that summary in the output's notes.
- Needs ffmpeg and ffprobe.

## 7. Checks before handing in

- The soundtrack is exactly the video's duration (within 0.05 s).
- The sound logo starts at `sound_logo_at`, is complete and unedited, and plays once.
- Loudness is within 1 LU of the target (the `measured` values in the mixer's summary).
- Every audio file used has a rights record that covers this use.
- Voice-over matches the approved script word for word.
