# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

First version, for internal testing.

- Workspace protocol, brand pack format with render tokens, and the CMO orchestrator with approval gates and budget control.
- Brand build and brand import, the brand guardian with its compliance rulebook, and `/cmo:review`.
- Strategist, researcher, storyteller, planner and copywriter agents; storytelling, channels and measurement knowledge; `/cmo:calendar` with calendar file export.
- Template contract and renderer: one master renders PNG or MP4 in every format, with subtitles, poster frames and a manifest. Starters: `announce-video` and `headline-card`.
- Designer, video producer and audio producer agents; the soundtrack mixer; `/cmo:templates`.
- Analyst agent, results data format, experiment calculator, `/cmo:report` and `/cmo:routine`.
- Connections rules, community manager agent, and `/cmo:publish` with hand-off, scheduler and Chrome routes, one yes per post.
- Team export and import with checksums.
- Hooks: session summary and automatic agent log.
- Deterministic test suite and model-graded evals with a fictional fixture brand.
